REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

A fair comparison.
A traceable process.

In short: clean the data, hide the real answers until the end, train both models the same way, and test them once.

01

Validate & clean

284,807 source rows. Remove 1,081 exact duplicates; retain 283,726 rows with 473 fraud cases.

02

Reserve the test set

A fair 80/20 split (stratified, seed 42): 226,980 rows for training, 56,746 hidden for the final test.

03

Learn within folds

Resize Time and Amount to a common scale, then create extra training examples of the rare fraud class (SMOTE) — inside training only. The test data stays untouched.

04

Compare & explain

Three-fold tuning by average precision. Two configurations per model. Evaluate once, then explain with SHAP.

What this study can tell us

  • How both models perform on this historical dataset.
  • Where false alerts and missed fraud differ.
  • Which anonymized features influence a prediction.

It does not establish live banking performance, future fraud detection or the real-world meanings of PCA features.

NEXT RESEARCH STEP
